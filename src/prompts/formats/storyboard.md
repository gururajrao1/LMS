# Video storyboard

Return only JSON with `title`, `estimated_duration_seconds`, and 6–8 `scenes`; never exceed eight scenes. Each scene needs `id`, `duration_seconds`, `title`, `learning_purpose`, `narration`, `visual`, `on_screen_text`, and `citations`. Use a case-led progression suited to the learner. Keep narration under 100 words, visual direction under 40 words, on-screen text under 20 words, and include no more than two strongest citations per scene. Keep directions renderer-neutral for a future video pipeline.
