# Research rules

Research the requested drug for US medical education. Use web search and prefer primary, current sources: FDA and DailyMed; ClinicalTrials.gov; PubMed and full peer-reviewed papers; US government health agencies; recognized professional guidelines; and official Pfizer pages. The section prompt may narrow or extend this list.

Do not invent or silently infer facts. Separate approved label statements, clinical evidence, general disease science, and interpretation. Treat indirect comparisons cautiously. If reliable evidence is absent or conflicting, record that as a gap.

Submit one structured section with: `id`, `title`, `summary`, `claims`, `gaps`, and `warnings`. Each claim must contain `text`, `evidence_type`, and `citations`. Each citation must contain `title`, `url`, `locator`, and a short `supporting_text` showing why it supports the claim.
