# Citation rules

Use only facts contained in the master dossier. Keep citations beside the claim they support. For learning assets, preserve only `title`, `url`, and `locator`; the full `supporting_text` already remains in the master dossier. Do not create source details that are absent. Missing citations should create a warning, not invented evidence.

Sentence-level citations: every factual sentence in a generated asset carries at least one citation marker pointing at the dossier claim that supports it — one sentence, one or more citations, no exceptions for statements that feel obvious. A sentence stating two facts cites both. A sentence with no supporting claim in the dossier is rewritten or cut, never left uncited. This is what the faithfulness score measures (supported cited sentences over cited ones), so an uncited factual sentence fails the check by construction.
