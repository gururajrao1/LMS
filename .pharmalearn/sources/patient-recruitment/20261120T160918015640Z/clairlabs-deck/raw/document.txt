Slide 6: HCP Outreach in Three Motions Digital affinity: NPI-targeted programmatic ads and geo-fencing around medical conferences KOL-based influence: key opinion leaders advocate through peer networks; physician-to-physician trust is the primary credibility driver Offline presence: clinics, centers of excellence, primary care, plus libraries, coffee shops, stadiums and billboards Complex and rare diseases lean hardest on KOL and offline presence
